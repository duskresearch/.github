**Creating things that should exist.**

Our best work starts after dusk, so the lab is named for the hour. Each project keeps its own name and its own repository; the lab is the quiet root they trace back to.

### Live

- **[Timebase](https://timebase.me)** · Visualize and plan across time zones. · [code](https://github.com/timebaseapp)
- **[Outage Observer](https://outage.observer)** · Live status for providers in your stack. · [code](https://github.com/duskresearch/outage-observer)
- **[Foogl](https://foo.gl)** · A link shortener you host yourself, on your own domain. · [code](https://github.com/duskresearch/foogl)
- **[Formweh](https://formweh.com)** · Forms, waitlists, and surveys made easy to self-host. · [code](https://github.com/duskresearch/formweh)
