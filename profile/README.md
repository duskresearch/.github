**We build tools and spaces that should exist.**

Our best work starts after dusk, so the lab is named for the hour. Some of what we make becomes a product, some stays an exploration, some turns into the next thing. Each keeps its own name and its own repository; the lab is the quiet root they trace back to.

### Live

- **[Timebase](https://timebase.me)** · Time zones and calendar countdowns, on iPhone and the web. · [code](https://github.com/timebaseapp)
- **[Outage Observer](https://outage.observer)** · Live status for the infrastructure and AI providers in your stack: a board, a bot, a Mac app. · [code](https://github.com/duskresearch/outage-observer)

### Nearly there

- **[Foogl](https://foo.gl)** · A link shortener you host yourself, on your own domain. · [code](https://github.com/duskresearch/foogl)
- **[Formweh](https://formweh.com)** · Forms, waitlists and surveys that land in one inbox you own. · [code](https://github.com/duskresearch/formweh)

### Exploring

- **[Infer](https://infer.fyi)** · An open, cross-platform AI browser. Local models first; your own key when you need more.
- **[Medivalent](https://medivalent.com)** · The equivalent medicine in another country, by substance rather than brand.
- **[Shio](https://shio.sh)** · An SSH client for iPhone, iPad and Mac, Tailscale-native. · [code](https://github.com/shio-sh/shio)
- **[Stem](https://stem.md)** · Document your rabbit holes; find trails worth following.

### Evolved

- **[Pasture](https://pasture.sh)** · Local AI chat on your iPhone, with Ollama on your Mac. It became Shio.
